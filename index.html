5b Rewind!

This is ARandomTaiwaneseGuy(YT)/ yuGesenawiaTmodnaRA(speedrun.com)/ RandTWGuy(Github)'s BFDIA 5b Rewind-er!
This can rewind time to any day in 5b speedrunning history and show the leaderboard at the time!
Displayed on a 2D grid leaderboard, every runner in the top 24 is shown, alongside profile pics and rank number! Slide the slider and watch historical events unfold, from the first ever run to KorZen v. AntLadders, from the infamous 9:51 WR to the rise of Numbly!
Still in testing phase! If you wish to add or correct your info, DM me on speedrun.com
THE AUTOPLAY FEATURE IS STILL IN TESTING. BEWARE OF WEIRD BUGS OVER THE NEXT FEW DAYS WITH IT.
Slide this slider to change the date! The canvas will display the Any% RTA PRs of each player at that date.

Note 1: Some runs have been timed only to the nearest second, some are only timed to the nearest 100ms, some at 30 fps, and some at 60 fps. I did not retime any runs; all runtimes are based on SRC data. Many early runs by MeesterTweester have not been timed to the millisecond, and so default to 0 milliseconds, etc.
Note 2: Player names are either speedrun.com or Youtube names, based solely on which one I'm more familliar with. (Except for "me, the dev") Speedrun.com usernames are the default, but if I'm more familliar with a player's YT account name, I will use that. For example, Trevarity and ader_farewell are YT account names. Other players, such as JohnsonJensonJason and 1000_percent_not_dead, are referred by nicknames, JJJ and 1000% respectively.
