5b Rewind!

This is ARandomTaiwaneseGuy(YT)/ yuGesenawiaTmodnaRA(speedrun.com)/ RandTWGuy(Github)'s BFDIA 5b Rewind-er!
This tool can rewind time to any day in 5b speedrunning history and show the leaderboard at the time!
Yes, speedrun.com does already have a time-constraint functionality, but hey, a little coding exercise never hurt anyone.
Plus, I think this 2D-displayer-thing will be cooler!
Note: Player names are either speedrun.com or Youtube names, based solely on which one I'm more familliar with. (Except for "me, the dev") Speedrun.com usernames are the default, but if I'm more familliar with a player's YT account name, I will use that. For example, Trevarity and ader_farewell are YT account names. Other players, such as JohnsonJensonJason and 1000_percent_not_dead, are referred by nicknames, JJJ and 1000% respectively.
Still in testing phase! If you wish to add or correct your info, DM me on speedrun.com
Slide this slider to change the date! The canvas will display the Any% RTA PRs of each player at that date.
